Frequently Asked Questions

How does the TF‑IDF approximation work?
It calculates term‑frequency divided by the total number of tokens in the supplied text. Since we only have one document, the inverse‑document‑frequency part is omitted, which is why we call it an “approximation”. It still highlights the most frequent, non‑stop‑word terms.
What is RAKE and why is it “simplified” here?
RAKE (Rapid Automatic Keyword Extraction) normally builds a graph of co‑occurring words. In this lightweight version we split the text into punctuation‑delimited phrases, strip stop‑words, and score a phrase by its length (number of words). It gives a quick, readable set of multi‑word keywords.
Can I use this page with large documents?
The demo runs entirely in the browser, so the only limit is the memory/CPU of the user’s device. For very large texts you may notice a short pause while the script tokenises and scores the words.
How is the summary generated?
The summary function extracts the first two‑to‑three sentences of the input (or up to 200 characters) as a quick “lead” summary. It’s a deterministic heuristic that works well for prose and news‑style text without requiring an external AI service.
